FOODNATI RESTAURANT PARTNER

Code of Conduct

Contents

Foodnati is committed to building a responsible, trustworthy, and professional food delivery platform for restaurants, customers, and Delivery Partners. This Code of Conduct establishes the standards expected from Restaurant Partners and their authorized staff while using Foodnati services. Restaurant Partners are expected to maintain food safety, accurate order handling, respectful communication, responsible business practices, and compliance with applicable laws.
