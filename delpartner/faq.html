Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining Foodnati.

Fill out the registration form on our website. Our team will guide you through the next steps.
You may need an Aadhaar card, PAN card, bank account details, and a valid driving licence if using a motor vehicle.
Flexible working options may be available depending on your location and delivery demand.
Earnings depend on delivery distance, applicable incentives, and the current partner payment structure.
Payment schedules will be explained during onboarding. Check with our team for the applicable payout cycle.
Vehicle requirements depend on the delivery options available in your area.
Delivery requests will appear in the Foodnati Delivery Partner app when you are available to accept orders.
